Those guys · Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Those guys is a desktop app for developers, made by Radhark. It runs on your computer. We don't run servers that receive your data.

Google Calendar

If you connect a Google account, the app reads your calendar list and events (title, time, guests, call link, description) so it can show upcoming meetings and fill in meetings you record. It only changes an event when you click "Add to event": then it adds your meeting summary to that event's description.

Calendar data and sign-in tokens stay on your computer; tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to Radhark or anyone else. You can disconnect an account in the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Those guys' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Meetings

Meeting audio is transcribed on your computer and not kept. Transcripts and summaries are files in your home folder. A summary is written by the AI agent you pick in the app (for example Claude or Codex, under your own account with them), which receives the transcript and, for calendar meetings, the event's title and guests' names.
